The secret isn't timing the market. It's not stopping.

People who try to buy the bottom usually miss it. People who put in a fixed amount every month buy more when prices drop and less when they rise, and end up paying an average price. The spreadsheet shows what happened to anyone who did that, over any period since 2010.

Look at the worst case: someone who started right at the top in November 2021.

That person spent 17 months in the red and saw -45% at the lowest point. Anyone who kept putting in $100 every month had put in $6,100 and, on 11/07/2026, would have $10,690.

The spreadsheet shows the bad periods too, on purpose. Over short periods Bitcoin can drop a lot and stay below what you paid for months or years. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
